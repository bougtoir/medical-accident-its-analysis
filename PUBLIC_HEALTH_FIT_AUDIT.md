# Public Health journal fit audit

Date: 2026-09-30. Source: official Guide for Authors, ScienceDirect
(https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/public-health/publish/guide-for-authors),
fetched in full via browser on 2026-09-30.

## A. Scope statements relevant to this paper

Public Health's published scope explicitly includes:

- "Health service effectiveness, management and re-design"
- "Capacity in public health systems and workforce"
- "Public health policy and comparisons"
- "Applied epidemiology"

The journal "will also be of interest to anyone involved in provision of public
health programmes, the care of populations or communities and those who
contribute to public health systems in any way." Health Policy and Technology's
editor suggested this journal specifically on scope grounds (automated Elsevier
scope match ~80% vs ~56% for HPT).

## B. Manuscript elements that already fit

- Original Research category: national longitudinal observational study of
  workforce capacity determinants — squarely within "Capacity in public health
  systems and workforce" and "Applied epidemiology".
- Reference style: Public Health uses AMA (style 6): superscript Arabic
  numerals in order of appearance, numbered reference list. The existing
  numbered-superscript citation apparatus carries over directly (brackets
  removed to match AMA).
- Structured abstract already uses labelled sections; remapped to the required
  Objectives / Study design / Methods / Results / Conclusions headings.
- Figures/tables: 2 tables + 2 figures within the 5-item manuscript cap;
  additional tables/figures moved to online supplementary, which the journal
  permits.
- Anonymised manuscript with a separate title page, matching the journal's
  double-blind-friendly preparation requirements.
- Declaration of Generative AI section follows the exact Elsevier wording
  required by the journal.

## C. Elements likely to trigger desk rejection (and what was done)

- Framing as a medico-legal/malpractice paper rather than a public-health
  workforce paper → title, abstract, introduction, discussion and conclusion
  were repositioned around specialty workforce supply, service capacity and
  workforce planning; litigation is presented as one candidate determinant.
- Word limit 3,000 (Original Research): the HPT version was ~3,112 words of
  main text → rewritten to ~2,663 (programmatic count, enforced in the build
  script, which now fails above 3,000).
- Abstract limit 250: rewritten to 188 words.
- "Papers ... will be referred back ... if they do not comply with Journal
  style" → separate title page, covering letter with the four required
  contents, separate declaration-of-interests document, figure/table placement
  markers ("[Table 1 near here]"), supplementary file.
- JOCS-CP causal-sounding interpretation ("structural policy effect") →
  downgraded to an explicitly exploratory association.
- Claims exceeding an ecological design ("physicians flee", "policies should
  focus on no-fault compensation") → replaced with bounded, design-calibrated
  statements.
- 26 references < 50 cap; British English enforced ("localised", "Anaesthesia").

## D. Concrete changes implemented

1. New title foregrounding supply/capacity and the equivalence design.
2. New structured abstract (Objectives/Study design/Methods/Results/Conclusions).
3. Introduction rebuilt around workforce planning + aims paragraph.
4. Discussion rewritten: principal finding → why equivalence matters →
   public-health workforce interpretation → international literature → Japan
   context → exploratory JOCS-CP → restrained policy implications →
   limitations → narrow conclusion.
5. Covering letter rewritten to Public Health's four required contents plus
   the permitted single transfer-provenance sentence.
6. Title page reformatted (initials/surname, degrees placeholder, institution,
   declaration of interests, corresponding-author contact fields).
7. Declaration-of-interests standalone document added (journal requires DoI in
   two places).
8. Highlights / Public Interest Summary removed (not required by this journal).
9. AMA superscript citations (bracket-free) in order of appearance.

## E. Formatting/submission requirements (verified)

- Article type: Original Research — ≤3,000 words, ≤5 tables/figures, ≤50 refs.
- Covering letter: why appropriate for Public Health; what is known; what the
  study adds; confirmation not published elsewhere — all present.
- Separate title page with title, authors' initials + surname, main degrees
  (two only), institution name/location, declaration of interests, and
  corresponding-author address/e-mail/telephone — present (degrees/e-mail/tel
  remain placeholders for the author to fill).
- Anonymised manuscript — no author identifiers in the body.
- Figures on separate sheets with legends; editable PPTX + TIFF (≥300 dpi)
  supplied; placement marked in text.
- Competing interests statement required even if none — present ("Declarations
  of interest: none" in title page AND separate document).
- Funding sentence uses the journal's standard wording.
- Generative AI declaration before References — present with required wording.
- Submission portal: www.editorialmanager.com/puhe (not the HPT transfer link;
  if the Elsevier transfer offer is accepted, files transfer automatically and
  this reformatted version can replace them on revision — decision for the user).

## F. Recommendation

**GO FOR PUBLIC HEALTH.** The verified scope ("Capacity in public health
systems and workforce") matches the paper's reframed contribution, and no hard
scope or format mismatch remains after the repositioning edits above.
