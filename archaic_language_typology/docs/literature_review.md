# Literature Review: Archaic Introgression and Language Typology

## Prior Work (Chronological)

### 1. Gene-Language Co-evolution (Global Scale)

| Reference | Key Finding | Method | Gap vs. Our Approach |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cavalli-Sforza et al. 1988 (PNAS) | Linguistic superfamilies correspond to two major genetic clusters (Caucasoid+E.Asian vs SE.Asian+Oceanian) | Classical markers (120 alleles, 42 pops), tree comparison | Used classical markers, not archaic introgression; no typological features |
| Cavalli-Sforza et al. 1992 (PNAS) | Gene-language tree congruence statistically significant | Permutation test on tree topology | Same as above |
| Hunley et al. 2015 (PNAS) | Reassessment: phonemic-genetic correlation weaker than claimed | Re-analysis of Atkinson 2011 | Doesn't address introgression |

### 2. Phonemic Diversity & Out-of-Africa

| Reference | Key Finding | Method | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Atkinson 2011 (Science) | Phoneme count declines with distance from Africa (serial founder effect) | Regression, 504 languages | Phoneme count only; no archaic introgression signal |
| Creanza et al. 2015 (PNAS) | Phonemic distance ~ genetic distance, but inventory SIZE doesn't follow founder effect | Procrustes, Mantel, 2082 languages + 246 pops | Overall genetic distance, not archaic-specific |

### 3. Specific Gene-Language Links

| Reference | Key Finding | Method | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dediu & Ladd 2007 (PNAS) | ASPM/Microcephalin haplogroup frequency correlates with tone languages | Partial correlation controlling geography | Two specific loci only; not archaic introgression genome-wide |
| Dediu & Ladd 2007 methodology | Novel: tested allele freq ~ typological feature (not just family) | | Our work extends this to archaic introgression proportions |

### 4. Regional Gene-Language Studies

| Reference | Key Finding | Method | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lansing et al. 2007 (PNAS, Sumba) | Genetic/linguistic distance significant even controlling geography (partial r=0.474, p=0.003) | Mantel + partial Mantel | Small island, single language family |
| Friedlaender et al. 2008 (PLOS Genet, Melanesia) | Gene-language co-evolution in Northern Island Melanesia | Matrix correlation | Regional, not global |
| Savage et al. 2021 (Sci Adv, NE Asia) | Grammar correlates with genetic history across 11 language families in NE Asia | PCA, Mantel, 14 pops | Regional; included genome-wide SNPs but not archaic-specific |
| Longobardi et al. 2020 (Genes, Finno-Ugric) | Syntactic distance parallels genetic distance in FU speakers | Syntactic parameters + aDNA | IE/FU/Altaic only; no archaic introgression |

### 5. FOXP2 and Archaic Deserts

| Reference | Key Finding | Relevance |
|---|---|---|
| Sankararaman et al. 2016 (Current Biology) | FOXP2 region is both Neanderthal AND Denisovan ancestry desert | Strong purifying selection removed archaic variants near speech/language genes |
| Enard et al. 2002 (Nature) | Two amino acid changes in FOXP2 fixed in modern humans | Language-specific selection |
| Krause et al. 2007 (Curr Biol) | Neanderthals share the same FOXP2 protein coding changes | Protein changes predate split; regulatory differences may matter |
| Crespi & Go 2022 (Curr Opin Behav Sci) | FOXP2 introgression desert implies functional incompatibility | Modern human language requires absence of archaic regulatory variants |

### 6. Wallace Line as Dual Boundary

| Reference | Key Finding | Relevance |
|---|---|---|
| Jacobs et al. 2019 (Cell) | Multiple Denisovan ancestries; one introgression event east of Wallace Line | Sharp genetic boundary |
| Papuan/Austronesian language boundary | Non-Austronesian (Papuan) languages concentrated east of Wallace Line | Linguistic boundary coincides with genetic boundary |

## Novelty of Current Proposal

**No prior study has tested whether archaic introgression levels (Neanderthal/Denisovan proportions) correlate with language TYPOLOGICAL features across global populations.**

Existing work either:
- Uses overall genetic distance (not archaic-specific)
- Uses language family membership (not typological features like morphological type)
- Is regional (not global)
- Tests specific candidate genes (ASPM/Microcephalin) rather than genome-wide archaic proportion

### Our Unique Contributions:
1. **Archaic introgression as deep-time migration tracer** → test if deep migration history predicts typological patterns
2. **Wallace Line test**: Formal statistical test of Denisovan introgression discontinuity coinciding with Austronesian/Papuan language boundary
3. **Morphological synthesis gradient**: Test agglutinating vs. fusional vs. isolating distribution against Neanderthal/Denisovan levels
4. **Partial Mantel**: Control for geographic distance to test whether archaic signal adds information beyond simple isolation-by-distance
5. **FOXP2 desert interpretation**: Frame the introgression desert as evidence that modern human linguistic capacity required removal of archaic regulatory variants
